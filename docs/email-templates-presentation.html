Key Design Decisions

FREQUENCY

Sent every Monday morning. Pledgers start their week seeing Paul's progress.

PERSONAL RUNNING TOTAL

The big orange number ($423.50) is the most important element. Watching it grow from $12 to $200 to $662 over 6 months creates ownership. It's their number.

TRAIL PROGRESS BAR

Visual representation of how far Paul has walked. Green-to-orange gradient matches the brand. Percentage gives concrete sense of completion.

JOURNAL EXCERPT

Emotional connection. A short quote from Paul's trail journal humanizes the numbers. Links to full entry on yeschapter.com to drive site traffic.

50/50 SPLIT SHOWN

Every email reinforces where the money goes: half to Tower Cancer Research, half to Cancer Council NSW. Builds trust.

DYNAMIC CONTENT

Every field is personalized: pledge rate, total, miles, days, journal excerpt. No two pledgers see the same email.

Key Design Decisions

FOREST GREEN HERO

Different color from weekly/milestone emails signals this is special. Green = completion, achievement, nature's finish line.

FINISH LINE PHOTO

Real photo from Manning Park. Emotional anchor — the pledger sees the moment their journey with Paul ends.

FINAL TOTAL — HUGE

$662.50 at 52px. This is the number they've watched grow for 6 months. Now it's real and it's time to act.

TWO FOUNDATION BUTTONS

Color-coded cards with pre-calculated amounts. Green for Tower Cancer Research (US), Orange for Cancer Council NSW (AU). Each has a full-width honor button linking directly to the foundation's donation page. No math, no confusion.

TAX DEDUCTION INFO

Each card shows the tax status: 501(c)(3) for US donors, DGR for Australian donors. Direct donations = cleanest tax receipts.

"I'VE HONORED" BUTTON

Self-report confirmation. Updates their profile, adds them to the Wall of Honor, changes their map bubble from grey to green.

INSTALLMENT OPTION

For large totals ($500+), honor over 3 months. Monthly reminders with 1/3 amount. Empathetic, not pushy.
